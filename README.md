# Loss Control Inspector

**Inspection scheduling, photo-evidence analysis and recommendation tracking for risk engineering.** Part of the [Zion AI App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/network.html) (Batch 101 — Insurance & Risk AI) by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com/).

## What it does
- **Smart inspection scheduling** — prioritize sites by exposure, loss history and recency.
- **Photo-evidence analysis** — AI vision flags hazards (electrical, housekeeping, egress) from field photos.
- **Recommendation tracking** — issue, chase and verify loss-control recommendations to closure.
- **Underwriting feedback loop** — inspection findings flow to [Underwriting Risk Radar](https://github.com/Zion-support/underwriting-risk-radar) at renewal.
